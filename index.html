Lyric Glow

Karaoke-style lyrics, a spinning vinyl and beat-reactive edge lighting for whatever you're listening to. Built from scratch, inspired by those desk-setup reels.

  • Word-by-word synced lyrics with 3D wheel, Insta pop, flip, slide and more
  • Vinyl with the album cover as the label, tonearm creeping across
  • Edge glow in the cover's colour that flashes on the beat
  • Reads Spotify or straight from your PC, no account needed
  • Transparent OBS/Discord overlay, screensaver mode, four themes

FreeCut

A free desktop video editor for Windows. CapCut-style layout, no watermark, no account, no subscription nagging you at export.

  • Multi-track timeline with keyframes, transitions and speed curves
  • Colour grading: curves, HSL, wheels, plus chroma key and masks
  • AI background removal and auto captions powered by Whisper
  • Text-to-speech, reverse, stabilise, presets and project autosave
  • Drag, scale and rotate clips right in the preview
